# SRHXtra is a supporter-run site about Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Following one IPL team across apps, aggregators and a timeline full of recycled takes is exhausting. So this site puts the whole season in one place — every game written up, every player profiled, the table, the photos, and a vote for player of the season. The numbers above are counted live from the site's own files, so they stay honest as the archive grows.

## What you'll find here

- Match coverage of every game from the 2026 campaign, written around what actually decided it.
- A preview before each game with the matchup and selection questions worth arguing about.
- A newsroom for squad moves, injuries and announcements.
- Profiles of every squad member with official headshots, batting and bowling styles, and 2026 numbers where they exist.
- A points table built from official IPL standings data.
- A player-of-the-season poll driven by one checked stat file.
- Photo albums of matchday frames from across the season.

## How we work

- Season numbers live in a single file where runs, balls and strike rates have to reconcile. If they don't add up, the page doesn't ship.
- Squad details and photos come from official IPL channels; player bios are the league's own profiles.
- Standings mirror the official IPL table.
- Mistakes still slip through. Flag them on our socials and they get fixed, not buried.

## What we're not

- Not affiliated with Sunrisers Hyderabad, the Sun Group, the IPL or the BCCI. This is a fan publication.
- Not a live-score app. Games get written up after the fact instead of rushed out ball by ball.
- Not a rumour mill. Unconfirmed stories stay out until they're confirmed.

## Who's behind it

SRHXtra is built and maintained by an SRH supporter who wanted the season covered properly. Say hello on X, Instagram and YouTube at @srhxtra.
