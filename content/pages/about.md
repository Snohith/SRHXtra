# SRHXtra is an independent editorial home for SRH supporters.

SRHXtra exists to cover Sunrisers Hyderabad with more intent than the average fan account and more clarity than the usual churn of copy-paste coverage.

## What we publish

- Fast-turnaround news when the squad, selection picture, or match context moves.
- Match coverage that focuses on turning points, tempo, and tactical decisions.
- A live points-table view built from official IPL standings data.
- Visual galleries that keep matchday atmosphere and fan moments part of the editorial package.

## Editorial approach

- Stay focused on SRH first.
- Add context before noise.
- Keep copy clean, fast, and readable on mobile.
- Favor clarity over hot takes and structure over filler.

## Publishing model

SRHXtra is built to stay lean: local content files, static deployment, and a workflow that lets one person publish consistently without adding a fragile backend stack.

That matters because the goal is not to run a complicated platform. The goal is to keep showing up with sharper SRH coverage all season.
