# Ishan Kishan's 270 puts East Zone on top in Duleep Trophy final

Ishan Kishan produced the defining innings of the Duleep Trophy final, making 270 as East Zone piled up 708 against South Zone in Chennai.

Resuming on 385 for four, with Kishan unbeaten on 111 and Kumar Kushagra on 63, East Zone continued to add runs at a steady rate on a surface that offered little to the bowlers.

Kushagra was the first to reach three figures. He brought up his century from 128 balls, the sixth of his first-class career, but was dismissed by Tripurana Vijay four deliveries later.

That left Kishan to carry much of the responsibility at the other end.

The East Zone captain moved through the gears without taking unnecessary risks. He reached 150 from 204 balls before turning his attention to the bowlers again.

His double century came from 270 deliveries, completed with a drive through the covers off Devdutt Padikkal. By then, the partnership had already put South Zone under sustained pressure.

Kishan's scoring rate changed noticeably after 200. He raced from 200 to 250 in just 31 balls, taking particular aim at the spinners and finding the boundary with increasing regularity.

He was forced to leave the field after reaching 250 but returned later in the innings after Mohammad Shami was dismissed. He added another 20 runs before Vijay finally ended his stay, with Kishan caught at long-on for 270.

The score left him three runs short of his first-class best of 273.

Kishan's knock included 30 fours and seven sixes. It also made him only the seventh batter to score a double hundred in a Duleep Trophy final and the first East Zone captain-wicketkeeper to do so.

East Zone's lower order made sure the big total kept growing.

Anukul Roy shared a 140-run partnership with Kishan after the earlier stand with Kushagra, while contributions from Md Kounain Qureshi and Shami pushed the side through the 700-run mark.

East Zone were eventually bowled out for 708 after 163 overs.

It was their first score above 600 in the Duleep Trophy and only the third time a side had crossed 700 in the final.

Kishan's 270 also stands as the second-highest individual score in a Duleep Trophy final, behind Raman Lamba's 320.

For South Zone, Vijay was the standout bowler with four wickets. The rest of the attack struggled to make consistent inroads, with Mohammed Siraj going wicketless after conceding 140 runs from his 25 overs.

By the time East Zone were dismissed, the match had been shaped around one innings. Kishan's long stay at the crease gave his team a huge first-innings advantage and left South Zone facing a daunting task when they came out to bat.
