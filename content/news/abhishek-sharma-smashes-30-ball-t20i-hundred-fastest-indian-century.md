# Abhishek Sharma smashes 30-ball T20I century against Afghanistan

Abhishek Sharma produced a stunning display of power hitting against Afghanistan, smashing a century from just 30 balls in the third T20I in New Delhi.

The India opener finished with 108 from 34 deliveries, hitting nine fours and 11 sixes. From the moment he walked in, Abhishek attacked the bowling and kept the pressure on Afghanistan.

He reached his fifty in only 14 balls and carried on in the same fashion. The Afghanistan bowlers struggled to find a length that could contain him as Abhishek kept picking out gaps and clearing the boundary.

His hundred came from his 30th delivery, making it the fastest T20I century by an Indian batter. He went past Rohit Sharma's earlier record of a 35-ball hundred, scored against Sri Lanka in 2017.

Abhishek's 30-ball hundred was also the fastest T20I century against a Test-playing nation.

The sixes kept coming as well. He hit 11 during his innings, adding to his reputation as one of India's most aggressive batters in the shortest format. His previous best in a T20I innings had been 13 sixes against England in Mumbai in 2025.

Abhishek was eventually dismissed for 108 off 34 balls, but by then he had already given India the kind of start that can completely change a T20 match.

The innings came at the end of an impressive series for the left-hander. He had scored 82 from 32 balls in the opening match and followed it with 39 in the second game.

He finished the three-match series with 229 runs at an average of 76.33 and a strike rate of 269.41. He also hit 22 sixes across the series.

His century earned him the Player of the Match award, while his overall performances across the series saw him named Player of the Series.
