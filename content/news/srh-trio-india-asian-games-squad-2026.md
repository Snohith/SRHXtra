## Three SRH players in India’s group

Abhishek Sharma, Ishan Kishan and Nitish Kumar Reddy have been selected in India’s 15-player squad for the men’s cricket competition at the 2026 Asian Games in Japan. The event is scheduled from 24 September to 3 October.

The three are also part of India’s current T20 picture: Abhishek and Ishan played in the opening Afghanistan T20I, while Nitish is retained in the updated India group subject to the squad announcement.

## SRH perspective

- Abhishek offers left-handed power at the top of the order.
- Kishan brings wicketkeeping and top-order flexibility.
- Nitish adds a seam-bowling all-round option to the middle order.
