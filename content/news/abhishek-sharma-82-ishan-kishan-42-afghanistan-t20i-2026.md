## SRH watch: Delhi T20I

Abhishek Sharma made 82 from 32 balls in India’s seven-wicket win over Afghanistan in the opening T20I in Delhi. His innings included seven fours and seven sixes.

Ishan Kishan contributed 42 at No. 3, and the two left-handers added 121 at speed to put India in control of the chase. The result gives SRH supporters a timely post-IPL marker: two of the side’s leading top-order options are carrying their attacking template into international cricket.

## What it means

- Abhishek’s performance followed a quieter run of T20I scores, but his stated approach remains to attack.
- Kishan’s role at No. 3 gave India a left-handed counterpoint immediately after the powerplay.
- The pair remain in India’s squad for the 2026 Asian Games, alongside SRH all-rounder Nitish Kumar Reddy.
