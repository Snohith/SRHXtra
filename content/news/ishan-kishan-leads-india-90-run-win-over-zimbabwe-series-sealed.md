Ishan Kishan gave India the platform and Tilak Varma supplied the late surge as India put Zimbabwe away by 90 runs in Harare to take an unassailable 2-0 lead in the T20I series.

Kishan's 81 from 44 balls was the innings that changed the game. He struck nine fours and two sixes, taking charge after India had lost two wickets inside the opening three overs.

Zimbabwe had won the toss and opted to field, hoping to make use of the conditions early. For a while, that decision appeared to be working. India lost Abhishek Sharma for eight before 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi departed for 20.

Kishan, though, settled the innings.

He first combined with captain Shreyas Iyer, adding 66 runs for the third wicket. Iyer made 25 before Brian Bennett broke the partnership.

That brought Varma to the middle, and Zimbabwe soon found themselves under serious pressure.

Kishan and Varma added 94 from only 43 deliveries. Kishan reached his half-century from 31 balls before eventually falling in the 18th over, but by then India were already closing in on a total beyond 200.

Varma continued attacking after Kishan's dismissal. The left-hander reached his fifty in just 23 balls and finished unbeaten on 60 from 29 deliveries, with five fours and three sixes.

India ended the innings at 219 for five.

Zimbabwe needed a fast start to stay in the contest, but India's bowlers kept taking wickets whenever the hosts threatened to build momentum.

Brian Bennett offered some resistance, scoring 32 from 19 balls, but his dismissal left Zimbabwe with too much ground to cover. Ryan Burl made 20 and Tadiwanashe Marumani scored 24, yet neither could produce the sustained partnership the chase required.

India's attack eventually wrapped up the innings in 17.5 overs.

Abhishek Sharma was the standout bowler, finishing with three wickets for 17 runs. Yash Thakur, making his T20I debut, picked up two wickets for 30, while Prince Yadav also claimed two before leaving the field with a hamstring problem.

The result completed India's series victory with one match still to play.

It was also an important win for Shreyas Iyer in his first T20I series as India's captain. After taking the opening match by seven wickets, India produced an even more convincing performance in the second game.

For Zimbabwe, there were few answers once India's batting accelerated. Their bowlers had India at 29 for two, but Kishan and Varma turned the game around before the Indian attack finished the job.

The third and final T20I will be played at the same Harare venue on July 26. India have already secured the series; Zimbabwe will now have one last chance to avoid a clean sweep.
