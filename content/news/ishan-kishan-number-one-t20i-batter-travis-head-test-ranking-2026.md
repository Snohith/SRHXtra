## Post-IPL ranking landmarks

Ishan Kishan became the No. 1 batter in the ICC Men’s T20I rankings after India’s series in Ireland, moving ahead of India teammate Abhishek Sharma. The rise followed a strong T20 World Cup campaign in which ICC reported 317 runs at a strike rate close to 200.

The same rankings update placed Travis Head at No. 1 in the ICC Men’s Test batting rankings for the first time. The two milestones underline the international reach of SRH’s left-handed batting core after the IPL season.
