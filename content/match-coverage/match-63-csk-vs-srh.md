# Match context

SRH's trip to Chepauk became a qualification night. Chennai Super Kings chose to bat, made 180/7, and SRH answered with 181/5 in 19 overs to win by five wickets. The result confirmed Sunrisers Hyderabad's playoff place and also locked Gujarat Titans into the knockouts.

The chase was the story because it was controlled rather than frantic. Ishan Kishan was named Player of the Match for 70 off 47 balls, while Heinrich Klaasen's 47 off 26 broke the pressure that CSK tried to build through the middle overs.

## Toss and team direction

CSK won the toss and batted first. Sanju Samson gave them early speed with 27 off 13, and Urvil Patel briefly pushed the tempo before Sakib Hussain removed him with a slower ball.

SRH's best response came through Pat Cummins. He removed Samson early, came back for Ruturaj Gaikwad, and finished with 3/28 to keep the innings from stretching beyond 180.

## First innings summary

Chennai had pockets of acceleration, but SRH kept landing wickets at the right time. Gaikwad's 15 off 21 slowed the middle, Kartik Sharma's 32 off 19 restarted the innings, and the Dewald Brevis-Shivam Dube stand gave CSK enough for a competitive total.

The death overs mattered. Brevis made 44 off 27 and Dube added 26, but SRH kept CSK to 180/7 instead of letting the innings become a 195-plus chase on a difficult Chepauk surface.

## Second innings summary

SRH's chase was built around Kishan's tempo control. He rotated early, punished loose balls, and gave the innings a stable center while Abhishek Sharma's 26 off 21 kept the asking rate manageable.

Klaasen then changed the required-rate picture. His 47 off 26, including repeated boundary pressure through the middle overs, meant SRH entered the closing stretch with room to absorb a couple of wickets and still finish in the 19th over.

## Tactical turning points

- Cummins' early wickets stopped CSK from turning their toss call into a runaway powerplay.
- SRH restricted the final total to 180 despite Brevis and Dube rebuilding for CSK.
- Kishan's 70 gave SRH a low-risk chase structure on a surface where collapses were possible.
- Klaasen's 47 off 26 pushed the match beyond CSK before the final over.

## Standout performers

- Ishan Kishan: 70 off 47 and Player of the Match.
- Heinrich Klaasen: 47 off 26 in the chase-defining middle phase.
- Pat Cummins: 3/28 to keep CSK within range.
- Dewald Brevis: 44 off 27 for Chennai's best batting contribution.

## Neutral closing takeaway

This was not SRH's loudest win of the season, but it may prove one of their most useful. They chased a tricky Chepauk target with enough control to confirm playoff qualification and keep their batting order looking settled at the business end of IPL 2026.
