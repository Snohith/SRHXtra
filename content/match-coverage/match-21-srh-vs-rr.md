# Match context

SRH's 57-run win over Rajasthan Royals on 13 April 2026 was built in two very different ways. First, Ishan Kishan converted an early wicket into a high-speed counterattack that lifted SRH to 216/6. Then SRH's debut seamers broke the chase so dramatically that RR spent most of the innings trying to recover from an unrecoverable position.

Rajasthan chose to field first in Hyderabad and briefly justified that call with a wicket from the opening ball. What followed was a much more decisive SRH response.

## Toss and team direction

Riyan Parag's decision to bowl looked sharp when Jofra Archer dismissed Abhishek Sharma first ball. SRH were immediately under pressure and needed someone to absorb that swing against the new ball rather than simply survive it.

Kishan supplied exactly that, but in attacking form rather than defensive form.

## First innings summary

Kishan's 91 from 44 transformed the innings. He scored at over 200, dominated the powerplay alongside Travis Head, and then built an 88-run partnership with Heinrich Klaasen that kept SRH in control of the middle overs.

Klaasen's 40 from 26 was important because it gave structure to the innings around Kishan's acceleration. Once Kishan fell short of a hundred, Nitish Kumar Reddy's 28 from 13 and Salil Arora's 24 not out from 13 kept the last overs productive enough to push SRH beyond 215.

The total of 216/6 was not only large; it was well distributed after the early setback, with SRH regaining control of both strike rate and wicket-loss rhythm.

## Second innings summary

The chase was effectively broken inside three overs. Praful Hinge's first over on debut removed Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, Dhruv Jurel, and Lhuan-dre Pretorius, making him the first bowler in IPL history to take three wickets in the opening over of an innings.

Sakib Hussain then removed Yashasvi Jaiswal for 1, and when Hinge returned to dismiss Parag, Rajasthan were 9/5. At that point the match had shifted from chase management to damage repair.

Donovan Ferreira and Ravindra Jadeja did provide serious resistance through a 118-run sixth-wicket partnership. Ferreira made 69 from 44 and Jadeja 45 from 32, which at least forced SRH to finish the job properly rather than coast home. But once Eshan Malinga removed Jadeja and Hussain came back to dismiss Ferreira, the innings folded at 159 in 19 overs.

## Tactical turning points

- Archer's first-ball wicket gave Rajasthan the ideal opening, but Kishan's counterattack erased that advantage quickly.
- The Kishan-Klaasen stand prevented Rajasthan from controlling the middle overs.
- Hinge's first over on debut changed the entire structure of the chase before it could settle.
- SRH still needed to break the Ferreira-Jadeja stand, and did so through Malinga and Hussain once the recovery had lasted deep into the innings.

## Standout performers

- Ishan Kishan's 91 from 44 was the innings that made 216 possible.
- Praful Hinge's opening over on debut was the defining bowling burst of the match.
- Sakib Hussain's wickets, including Ferreira late, ensured RR's recovery never became a real chase.
- Donovan Ferreira's 69 was the main reason the margin did not become even larger.

## Final result

Sunrisers Hyderabad beat Rajasthan Royals by 57 runs. SRH scored 216/6 and bowled RR out for 159 in 19 overs.

## Neutral conclusion

This was one of SRH's clearest wins because they were better at both reset moments of the game. Kishan turned an early batting setback into scoring control, and the debut seamers converted a large defense into a chase collapse before Rajasthan could construct a stable response.
