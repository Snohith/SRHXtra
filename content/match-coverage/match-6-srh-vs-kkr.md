# Match context

SRH's 65-run win over Kolkata Knight Riders on 2 April 2026 was the clearest early-season example of their intended balance: explosive top-order scoring followed by enough bowling discipline to defend a large total.

At Eden Gardens, KKR chose to field first and still found themselves chasing 227 after SRH made 226/8. The second half of the match mattered just as much as the first because SRH became the first side in IPL 2026 to defend a total.

## Toss and team direction

Ajinkya Rahane won the toss in his 200th IPL appearance and opted to chase. That decision placed the pressure on SRH's openers to avoid a new-ball slowdown, and they answered by making the powerplay the decisive stretch of the innings.

## First innings summary

Travis Head and Abhishek Sharma forced KKR immediately onto the back foot. SRH reached 84/1 in the powerplay, with Abhishek making 48 off 21 and Head 46 off 21. The innings' tone was set by how aggressively SRH took on Varun Chakaravarthy rather than letting him dictate the middle overs.

Once the openers had created the platform, Heinrich Klaasen shifted into control mode. His 52 from 35 was less about raw acceleration than about making sure the innings did not stall after the opening burst. Nitish Kumar Reddy's 39 from 24 provided the next wave of scoring and kept SRH tracking toward a total above 220.

Blessing Muzarabani's 4/41 prevented the innings from becoming even bigger, but SRH still closed on 226/8 because every time KKR appeared to regain some order, another partnership carried the score forward.

## Second innings summary

KKR's chase began with a short, violent burst from Finn Allen, who made 28 from 7. That start could have turned the chase into a direct counterpunch, but Harsh Dubey's dismissal of Allen broke the immediate surge.

Angkrish Raghuvanshi's 52 from 29 and Rinku Singh's 35 from 25 kept KKR in the game for stretches, yet the innings never found stable continuity. SRH combined wickets with fielding pressure, and the reported mix-ups between wickets magnified that pressure once KKR slipped behind the asking rate.

Jaydev Unadkat's 3/21 was the cleanest summary of SRH's bowling control, while Nitish Kumar Reddy's 2/17 and Eshan Malinga's 2/14 helped finish the chase at 161 in 16 overs.

## Tactical turning points

- SRH's 84/1 powerplay meant KKR were chasing the game before the middle overs had even started.
- The attack on Varun Chakaravarthy removed a key defensive option from KKR's plans.
- Harsh Dubey dismissing Finn Allen stopped the chase from building on its first overs.
- SRH's fielding pressure and bowling changes prevented KKR from assembling a long middle-overs partnership.

## Standout performers

- Heinrich Klaasen's 52 from 35 gave structure to an innings that could otherwise have become top-heavy.
- Nitish Kumar Reddy influenced both innings, adding 39 from 24 and taking 2/17.
- Jaydev Unadkat's 3/21 was central once the required rate began to climb.
- Blessing Muzarabani's 4/41 was KKR's main resistance with the ball.

## Final result

Sunrisers Hyderabad beat Kolkata Knight Riders by 65 runs. SRH scored 226/8 and bowled KKR out for 161 in 16 overs.

## Neutral conclusion

This was one of SRH's more complete outings because the batting burst was followed by control rather than drift. A 220-plus total alone did not decide the game; the bowling and fielding made sure KKR never converted their own fast start into a sustained chase.
