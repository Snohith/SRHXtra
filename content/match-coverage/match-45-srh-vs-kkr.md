# Match context

SRH won the toss and chose to bat in Hyderabad, but Kolkata Knight Riders turned the match through the middle overs. SRH were bowled out for 165 in 19 overs, and KKR reached 169/3 in 18.2 overs to win by seven wickets.

The official report points to one clear hinge: SRH were 105/1 in the ninth over before losing 9 wickets for 60 runs. KKR's chase was then controlled by Angkrish Raghuvanshi's half-century and support from Ajinkya Rahane.

## Toss and team direction

Pat Cummins chose to bat first on a dry afternoon surface. The decision had early support because Travis Head and Ishan Kishan put SRH in a strong position before KKR's spinners took the innings away.

Once SRH were bowled out for 165, the match changed shape. The target gave KKR room to absorb wickets rather than chase at extreme risk.

## First innings summary

Head made 61 off 28 balls, striking 9 fours and 3 sixes, while Ishan added 42 off 29. At 105/1, SRH looked set for a far larger first-innings score.

Varun Chakaravarthy changed that trajectory with 3/36, supported by Sunil Narine's 2/31. The official report also notes KKR's fielding impact, including Rovman Powell's catch, before Kartik Tyagi's 2/30 helped finish the innings at 165.

## Second innings summary

SRH needed early wickets, but Finn Allen's 29 off 13 gave KKR a fast start. Rahane then made 43 off 36, giving the chase a steadier middle phase.

Raghuvanshi finished as KKR's anchor with 59 off 47 balls. Rinku Singh's unbeaten 22 off 11 provided the final push, and KKR finished the chase with 10 balls remaining.

## Tactical turning points

- SRH's 105/1 platform had control, but the 9-for-60 collapse defined the match.
- Varun's 3/36 and Narine's 2/31 made KKR's spin overs decisive.
- Finn Allen's powerplay scoring prevented SRH from defending 165 through early pressure.
- Raghuvanshi and Rahane kept the chase stable enough for Rinku to finish it without a late scramble.

## Standout performers

- Official player of the match: Varun Chakaravarthy.
- Varun Chakaravarthy: 3/36.

This was the first interruption to SRH's winning run after five straight victories. The warning sign was specific rather than vague: a strong top-order platform became 165 all out, leaving the bowlers with too little room once KKR started the chase cleanly.
