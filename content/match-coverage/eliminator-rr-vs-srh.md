# Match context

SRH's IPL 2026 season ended in New Chandigarh. Sunrisers Hyderabad won the toss and chose to field, but Rajasthan Royals made 243/8 and then bowled SRH out for 196 in 19.2 overs to win the Eliminator by 47 runs and qualify for Qualifier 2.

The match was shaped by one extraordinary innings. Vaibhav Sooryavanshi made 97 off 29 balls, hit 12 sixes, and was named Player of the Match after forcing SRH into a chase that had almost no margin for error.

## Toss and team direction

SRH chose to field first, backing their chase profile and hoping to control the early RR surge. That plan broke almost immediately because Rajasthan attacked the powerplay without pause.

Sooryavanshi's innings changed the tactical map. Length balls disappeared, pace changes did not buy enough control, and SRH were pushed into recovery mode before the first innings had settled.

## First innings summary

RR's 243/8 came from a powerplay that SRH could not contain and a middle phase that kept the pressure high. Sooryavanshi's 97 included five fours and 12 sixes, with eight of those sixes coming in the powerplay.

Dhruv Jurel added a 21-ball fifty to make sure the innings did not stall after the initial burst. SRH did pull the scoring back late, conceding only 29 runs in the final four overs, but the damage had already put Rajasthan in command.

## Second innings summary

SRH had to attack from ball one and paid for it. Jofra Archer removed the in-form top three, including Abhishek Sharma for a duck in his first over, leaving the chase under immediate stress.

Nitish Kumar Reddy and Impact Player Salil Arora briefly reopened the match with a fifty-run stand off 19 balls. At the halfway point SRH still needed 112 from 60, which was possible only with sustained boundary hitting. RR kept taking enough wickets to stop that rhythm, and SRH were all out for 196.

## Tactical turning points

- Sooryavanshi's powerplay hitting forced SRH away from their first-choice bowling plans.
- RR still reached 243 despite SRH conceding only 29 runs in the final four overs.
- Archer's new-ball wickets removed SRH's top-order platform before the chase could breathe.
- The Nitish-Arora counterattack gave SRH hope, but Rajasthan kept the wicket column moving.

## Standout performers

- Vaibhav Sooryavanshi: 97 off 29 and Player of the Match.
- Dhruv Jurel: a 21-ball fifty that kept RR's innings moving.
- Jofra Archer: new-ball wickets that broke SRH's chase structure.
- Nitish Kumar Reddy and Salil Arora: a rapid fifty-run partnership that briefly revived SRH.

## Neutral closing takeaway

SRH were beaten by the sort of playoff innings that changes every plan around it. Their late first-innings recovery and mid-chase counterattack showed fight, but RR's early batting violence and new-ball wickets made the gap too large to close.
