# Match context

SRH's third straight win came with their cleanest batting statement of the season. Delhi Capitals won the toss on 21 April 2026 and chose to field in Hyderabad, but SRH used that invitation to build a 242/2 total and then defend it by 47 runs.

The result was shaped by Abhishek Sharma's unbeaten 135 off 68 balls, a late Heinrich Klaasen surge, and a bowling finish led by Eshan Malinga. DC reached 195/9, which kept the chase alive in bursts without ever matching the pressure created by SRH's first innings.

## Toss and team direction

Axar Patel sent SRH in first, looking for early movement from the Delhi seamers. SRH's response was to take control before the field could fully spread, with Travis Head making 37 off 26 and Abhishek carrying the scoring burden through the innings.

The innings never needed a rescue phase. It needed acceleration, and SRH found it twice: first through the opening pair, then through Klaasen's unbeaten 37 off 13 balls at the death.

## First innings summary

Abhishek's innings gave SRH both volume and tempo. He finished unbeaten on 135 from 68 deliveries, with 10 fours and 10 sixes, and turned a strong platform into a 242/2 scoreboard.

Head's 37 gave the start structure, Ishan Kishan added 25 off 13, and Klaasen's late hitting pushed the total beyond Delhi's comfort range. The unbroken Abhishek-Klaasen stand made the final overs decisive.

## Second innings summary

Delhi needed a record-paced chase, but SRH kept finding wickets at the right moments. Dilshan Madushanka struck early, KL Rahul made 37 off 23, and Nitish Rana's 57 off 30 became DC's strongest answer through the middle.

Sameer Rizvi made 41 and Tristan Stubbs added 27, but the target forced Delhi into risk before they could build a settled partnership. Malinga's 4/32 and Harsh Dubey's 3/12 kept the chase to 195/9.

## Tactical turning points

- Abhishek's hundred changed the ceiling of the match, taking SRH from a strong start to a total beyond 240.
- Klaasen's 37 off 13 made the death overs decisive rather than merely productive.
- Madushanka's early wicket stopped DC from matching SRH's opening rhythm.
- Malinga's four-wicket spell broke the middle and lower order whenever the chase threatened to reconnect.

## Standout performers

- Official player of the match: Abhishek Sharma.
- Abhishek Sharma: 135* off 68 balls, with 10 fours and 10 sixes.
- Heinrich Klaasen: 37* off 13 balls.
- Eshan Malinga: 4/32 in four overs.
- Harsh Dubey: 3/12 in two overs.

## Neutral closing takeaway

This was not just a batting blowout. SRH paired their highest-impact innings of the campaign with enough wicket-taking discipline to keep Delhi from turning a huge chase into a late scramble.
