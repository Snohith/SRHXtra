# Match context

SRH won the toss, chose to field, and still had to complete one of the season's bigger chases after Rajasthan Royals reached 228/6 in Jaipur. The chase finished at 229/5 in 18.3 overs, giving SRH a five-wicket win with nine balls unused.

The match was shaped by two very different batting innings. Rajasthan leaned heavily on Vaibhav Sooryavanshi's 103 off 37 balls, while SRH spread the chase across Ishan Kishan, Abhishek Sharma, Heinrich Klaasen, and Nitish Kumar Reddy.

## Toss and team direction

SRH's decision to bowl first did not produce early control. Rajasthan were 76/1 after the powerplay, with Sooryavanshi setting a pace that briefly pointed toward a total beyond 240.

The later overs pulled the innings back into a more reachable range. RR still finished with 228, but the official report notes that wickets at regular intervals and a quieter finish kept the total below what their start had threatened.

## First innings summary

Sooryavanshi was the main reason Rajasthan reached 228/6. His 103 off 37 balls gave the innings its speed, and Dhruv Jurel added a half-century in support.

Beyond those two contributions, RR did not sustain the same scoring pressure through the back half. That mattered because a 228 target at Jaipur still left SRH a chaseable equation if the top order matched Rajasthan's tempo.

## Second innings summary

SRH lost Travis Head early to Jofra Archer, but Abhishek and Ishan changed the chase quickly. They took SRH to 89/1 by the end of the powerplay, putting the required rate under control instead of letting the target dictate the innings.

Abhishek made 57 off 29 balls and Ishan, the official player of the match, made 74 off 31. Klaasen then steadied the chase after the set batters fell, and Nitish Kumar Reddy's 36 off 18 helped SRH close the game at 229/5.

## Tactical turning points

- Rajasthan's 76/1 powerplay gave the first innings its platform, but SRH limited the final total by taking wickets through the second half.
- Sooryavanshi's hundred gave RR a large score without turning it into an unreachable one.
- SRH's 89/1 powerplay changed the tone of the chase after Head's early dismissal.
- Klaasen and Nitish kept the finish orderly once Abhishek and Ishan were out.

## Standout performers

- Official player of the match: Ishan Kishan.
- Ishan Kishan: 74 off 31 balls.
- Abhishek Sharma: 57 off 29 balls.
- Vaibhav Sooryavanshi: 103 off 37 balls for Rajasthan Royals.
- Nitish Kumar Reddy: 36 off 18 balls in the chase.

## Neutral closing takeaway

This was a batting-led win rather than a bowling correction. Rajasthan created the headline innings through Sooryavanshi, but SRH answered with a broader chase and enough control after the powerplay to make 229 look managed rather than desperate.
