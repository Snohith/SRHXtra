# Match context

SRH lost a game that split sharply into two different batting stories. The first was survival and recovery after a severe top-order collapse. The second was whether 156 could still become enough once the surface slowed and the chase tightened.

Lucknow Super Giants won the toss on 5 April 2026 and chose to field in Hyderabad. They then held their nerve better at both ends of the game, chasing 157 with one ball to spare for a five-wicket win.

## Toss and team direction

LSG's choice to bowl first was built around their seam attack, and Mohammad Shami gave them exactly the opening spell they needed. SRH were 11 for 3 inside four overs after Abhishek Sharma, Travis Head, and Ishan Kishan were removed.

That start forced SRH away from their preferred pattern of powerplay aggression and into a low-risk rebuild.

## First innings summary

The innings looked close to irrecoverable when Liam Livingstone also fell and SRH slipped to 35 for 4 after 10 overs. At that stage, the immediate task was not acceleration but simply reaching a defendable total.

Heinrich Klaasen and Nitish Kumar Reddy changed the match's shape with a 116-run fifth-wicket partnership, a new SRH record for that wicket in the IPL. Klaasen made 62 from 41 and Reddy 56 from 33, and the pair shifted gears decisively from the 14th over onward.

That acceleration brought SRH back to a competitive zone, but the finish undermined it. Once M. Siddharth dismissed Reddy in the 18th over, SRH lost five wickets for 22 runs in the final three overs and closed on 156/9 rather than the stronger total that partnership had threatened.

## Second innings summary

LSG's chase began positively through Mitchell Marsh and Aiden Markram, with Markram's 45 from 27 setting the tempo after Marsh fell early. SRH did create a middle phase where the game tightened: Shivang Kumar dismissed Markram, and Nicholas Pooran and Ayush Badoni both fell before the target was fully secure.

From there, Rishabh Pant's control became the difference. He did not chase the finish too early, but he kept the innings stable through the uncertainty and reached an unbeaten 68 from 50.

SRH dragged the contest into the final over, leaving LSG nine to get, but Pant settled it himself with three boundaries off Jaydev Unadkat's last over. The chase ended at 160/5 in 19.5 overs.

## Tactical turning points

- Shami's opening spell turned SRH's first ten overs into damage limitation.
- Klaasen and Reddy rebuilt the innings well enough to create a realistic defense.
- SRH's late collapse changed the target from potentially robust to merely competitive.
- Pant's measured chase management meant LSG did not panic when SRH briefly squeezed the middle overs.

## Standout performers

- Heinrich Klaasen's 62 and Nitish Kumar Reddy's 56 rescued SRH from 35 for 4.
- Mohammad Shami's 2/9 in four overs drove LSG's powerplay control.
- Aiden Markram's 45 gave LSG a stable start.
- Rishabh Pant's unbeaten 68 completed the chase under late pressure.

## Final result

Lucknow Super Giants beat Sunrisers Hyderabad by 5 wickets. SRH scored 156/9 and LSG replied with 160/5 in 19.5 overs.

## Neutral conclusion

SRH's middle-order recovery deserved a tighter finish than it received. The decisive gap was not only the top-order collapse, but the late loss of five wickets for 22 that turned a hard chase into a manageable one for a side that still had Pant in control.
