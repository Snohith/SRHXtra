# Match context

SRH closed their league-stage home slate with a 55-run win over Royal Challengers Bengaluru. Pat Cummins chose to bat first, SRH made 255/4, and RCB finished at 200/4 after the chase lost shape through the middle overs.

The result did not stop RCB from holding a top-two finish, but it gave SRH a sharp batting statement before the playoffs. Ishan Kishan was named Player of the Match for 79 off 46 balls, while Abhishek Sharma and Heinrich Klaasen supplied the acceleration around him.

## Toss and team direction

SRH won the toss and batted first on a surface that rewarded clean hitting. Travis Head made 26 off 16, but Abhishek Sharma was the player who turned the powerplay into an SRH launch phase.

RCB needed early wickets to keep the total in range. Rasikh Salam eventually removed Head and Kishan, but by then SRH had already built the base for a 250-plus score.

## First innings summary

Abhishek's 56 off 22 set the tone. His powerplay hitting forced RCB into defensive fields early, and that opened the next phase for Kishan to control the innings rather than chase it.

Kishan's 79 off 46 gave SRH the anchor, Klaasen's 51 off 24 kept the innings at a punishing speed, and Nitish Kumar Reddy's unbeaten 29 off 12 finished the job. SRH reached 255/4, a total that made RCB chase near-perfect cricket.

## Second innings summary

RCB started with intent through Venkatesh Iyer's 44 off 19, but the required rate remained severe. Virat Kohli made 15, Rajat Patidar fought for 56 off 39, and Krunal Pandya's unbeaten 41 kept the score respectable.

SRH's bowling did enough because it avoided the one catastrophic over that a 256 chase needs. Eshan Malinga returned 2/33, Sakib Hussain gave away only 31 in his four overs, and the middle overs left RCB too far behind.

## Tactical turning points

- Abhishek Sharma's 56 off 22 made the powerplay SRH's most important scoring phase.
- Kishan and Klaasen ensured there was no slowdown after the opening burst.
- Nitish Kumar Reddy's 29 not out from 12 balls lifted the total into a range that changed the chase psychology.
- Malinga's wickets and SRH's slower-ball control prevented RCB from sustaining their early powerplay rate.

## Standout performers

- Ishan Kishan: 79 off 46 and Player of the Match.
- Abhishek Sharma: 56 off 22 to ignite SRH's innings.
- Heinrich Klaasen: 51 off 24 through the middle and death overs.
- Eshan Malinga: 2/33 in a match where most bowlers were under pressure.

## Neutral closing takeaway

This was a batting-heavy win, but not only a batting win. SRH used the toss well, built a total that demanded risk, and then bowled just tightly enough to keep RCB from turning a difficult chase into a real scare.
