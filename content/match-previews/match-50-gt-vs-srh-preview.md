# The next test

SRH travel to Ahmedabad with the table finally reflecting the force of their best cricket. The 33-run win over Punjab Kings put Sunrisers Hyderabad at the top, but the next fixture asks a different question: can that top-table momentum travel to the Narendra Modi Stadium against Gujarat Titans?

The match is set for 12 May 2026 at 19:30 IST. It arrives at a useful point in the season, with SRH trying to protect momentum, net run rate, and playoff positioning rather than simply chase a rebound.

## Match details

- Fixture: Gujarat Titans vs Sunrisers Hyderabad
- Date: 12 May 2026
- Time: 19:30 IST
- Venue: Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad
- Context: SRH enter after moving to the top of the IPL 2026 points table.

## Powerplay: SRH intent vs GT new-ball plans

The first tactical hinge is obvious. SRH's ceiling still starts with Abhishek Sharma and Travis Head turning the first six overs into a field-setting problem. Against PBKS, they again showed how quickly a match can tilt when both openers access pace and width early.

GT's new-ball plan will need to be calmer than reactive. The danger for any opponent is overcorrecting after the first boundary burst. If Gujarat can keep one end quiet and force SRH to manufacture shots, the innings can be slowed before Klaasen and Ishan enter their preferred rhythm. If not, SRH can make Ahmedabad feel like Hyderabad very quickly.

## Middle overs: Ishan and Klaasen hold the shape

The PBKS win was important because Ishan Kishan and Heinrich Klaasen did not only rescue or finish; they controlled the middle. Their 88-run partnership made SRH less dependent on the openers being perfect.

That role becomes crucial away from home. Ishan's tempo against spin and Klaasen's ability to hit pace or spin without needing a long runway give SRH two different ways to protect the innings. The aim will be to avoid a soft reset after the powerplay and keep GT defending boundary options through overs 7 to 15.

## Bowling matchups: Cummins' control travels

Pat Cummins' early wickets against PBKS changed the chase before Cooper Connolly could fully define it. In Ahmedabad, SRH will again need Cummins to set the tone, especially if the surface rewards clean hitting once batters settle.

The supporting cast matters just as much. Eshan Malinga's pace through the middle and Shivang Kumar's wicket-taking value give SRH ways to break partnerships without waiting for the death overs. The key will be discipline after the first breakthrough: GT cannot be allowed a low-risk rebuilding phase.

## What SRH need to protect

The table position is useful, but it also changes the pressure. SRH are no longer only building a run; they are defending a place. That means NRR, chase management, and bowling economy all matter as much as the win column.

For SRH, the cleanest route is familiar: win the powerplay without emptying the middle order, keep Klaasen involved deep enough to shape the finish, and make Cummins' overs count before GT's chase can settle.

## SRHXtra read

Ahmedabad is a momentum test more than a personality test. SRH already know who they are when their batting starts fast. The sharper question is whether they can carry the same balance away from Hyderabad: aggressive enough to dictate, controlled enough to protect the top-table position they have earned.
